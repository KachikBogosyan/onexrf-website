# **Dilator Tipping**
### *Precision Taper Formation for Smooth, Reliable Dilator Insertion*

Dilator tips must deliver consistent geometry, smooth insertion, and perfect compatibility with matching sheaths. Variations in polymer type, wall thickness, and and taper profile make dilator tipping one of the most challenging catheter-forming applications.

**ONEXRF specializes in high-precision dilator tapering**, delivering repeatable, production-ready results across all medical-grade thermoplastic materials.
